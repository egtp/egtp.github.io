EGTP is for everyone. We want every speaker, attendee, volunteer and staff member to feel welcome, comfortable and safe.

## Be generous

Treat people with respect. Ask before taking photographs, use the names and pronouns people share, and make room for different experiences and points of view. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation and unwanted physical contact are not welcome.

## Need support?

Please speak with an EGTP or Sydney Games Festival staff member at the event. We will listen, respond with care and take appropriate action.
